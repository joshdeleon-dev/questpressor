How it works

Live before your next morning rush.

  1. 01

    Set up your café

    Add your name, logo and colors, then pick a reward from a template, like "Buy 9, get the 10th free."

  2. 02

    Put up your QR code

    Print it for the counter. Customers scan it with their phone camera and add the card to their wallet in a few seconds.

  3. 03

    Stamp and watch them return

    Staff add stamps from any phone or tablet. Customers get updates on their lock screen, and you see the trends.

Questions, answered

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They scan your QR code with their phone camera and the card saves straight to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

What does it cost?

Your first café will be free on the core plan. Paid plans for multiple locations and advanced features will be announced at launch, and founding cafés get a discount.

What equipment do I need?

Just the phone or tablet you already use. Staff open the QuestPresso app to scan a customer’s card and add a stamp.

Can I change my rewards later?

Yes. Edit your offer at any time and every customer’s card updates automatically.
